Common questions

How do I play? Compare the two pictures and tap anything that's different. There are 5 changes in every scene, and the one marked with a gold diamond is evidence for the case. Pinch to zoom in.

I'm stuck on a scene. Tap Hint to circle a difference. Hints are free with a short video or cost coins.

How do I solve a case? Search all 10 scenes of a case, then tap Name the killer. Choose a suspect and the 3 strongest pieces of evidence. A wrong guess just lets you try again.

I bought Remove Ads on another device. Open Settings → Restore purchases (or Shop → Restore purchases) while signed in with the same Apple ID.

The music is too much. Turn music or sound effects off in Settings, or from the pause menu during a scene. The game also respects your iPhone's silent switch.
