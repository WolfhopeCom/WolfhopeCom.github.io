Common questions

How do I restore Lumi Plus on a new phone?

Tap the gear on the island to open Settings, then tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel Lumi Plus?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Lumi Isle. You can also tap Manage subscription in the App's Settings.

Can I change when Lumi reminds me?

Reminders follow the check-in time in your plan (morning, evening or both). Turn them on or off in the App's Settings.

Will I lose my island if I switch phones?

No. If you're signed in to iCloud, Lumi Isle keeps a copy of your island in your private iCloud. Install the App on your new phone with the same Apple Account and it comes right back. It's also included in your regular iPhone backups.

How do friends work?

Turn on Friends from the You tab. Lumi Isle uses Game Center, so your Game Center friends who play show up automatically. Tap + Invite to add someone new. Friends see your companion, island level and whether you checked in today, never your goals, moods or journal. Turn it off anytime in Settings.

I missed a few days. Did I lose progress?

Never. Nothing on the island is ever lost. Lumi is just happy to see you.
