Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

The short version: There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your plan, goals, moods and journal never leave your device and your own iCloud. If you choose to turn on Friends, a small profile of your companion is shared with your Game Center friends. That's it.

What stays on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the App ("Lumi Isle") saves your name and your companion's name, your plan and goals, check-ins, moods and reflections, your island, village and treasures, and your settings. This is stored only in the App's storage on your device, with a backup copy in the App's own files so iOS can't clear it. It is never sent to us. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy is also kept in your own private iCloud storage so your island is safe if you get a new phone or reinstall the App. That copy is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy terms, and we can't see it. Device backups you make through iCloud or a computer may include it too.

Widget and reminders

The home screen widget shows a small summary (your companion's name, today's goal count and light) that is shared only between the App and its widget on your device. Daily check-in reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off anytime in the App's Settings or in iPhone Settings.

Friends (optional)

Friends is off until you turn it on. When it's on, the App uses Apple's Game Center to find your Game Center friends who also play. To show them your companion, the App stores a small profile in the App's iCloud database: your Game Center player ID, your companion's name and look (color, outfit, growth stage), your island's name, level and number of villagers, and the date you last checked in. When you send a friend a "good vibe," the App stores who sent it, who it's for, which vibe it was and when. Your goals, moods, reflections and journal are never shared.

The App only shows your profile to your Game Center friends who also use the App, and vibes from people who aren't your friends are ignored. Turning Friends off (in Settings) deletes your profile. Game Center is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What we don't collect

Apart from the optional Friends profile above, we don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, health data, device identifiers or usage data. The App doesn't include advertising, analytics or tracking software from other companies, and everything except Friends works without an internet connection.

Purchases

Lumi Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple's App Store under Apple's privacy policy. The App receives only a confirmation from Apple, on your device, of whether your subscription is active. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Children

The App is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Not medical care

Lumi Isle supports everyday self-care. It isn't therapy or medical treatment. If things feel like too much, free and confidential support in your country is listed at findahelpline.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
