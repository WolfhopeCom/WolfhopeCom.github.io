Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

The short version: Lumi Isle doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your plan, moods and reflections stay on your device.

What stays on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the App ("Lumi Isle") saves your name and your companion's name, your plan and goals, check-ins, moods and reflections, your island, village and treasures, and your settings. This is stored only in the App's storage on your device, with a backup copy in the App's own files so iOS can't clear it. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the App. Device backups you make through iCloud or a computer may include it, under Apple's privacy terms.

Widget and reminders

The home screen widget shows a small summary (your companion's name, today's goal count and light) that is shared only between the App and its widget on your device. Daily check-in reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off anytime in the App's Settings or in iPhone Settings.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your email, location, contacts, photos, health data, device identifiers or usage data. The App doesn't include advertising, analytics or tracking software from other companies, and it works without an internet connection.

Purchases

Lumi Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple's App Store under Apple's privacy policy. The App receives only a confirmation from Apple, on your device, of whether your subscription is active. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Children

The App is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Not medical care

Lumi Isle supports everyday self-care. It isn't therapy or medical treatment. If things feel like too much, free and confidential support in your country is listed at findahelpline.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
