Lumi Isle

A cozy island that grows with you

A small sprout named Lumi wakes up on a faded island. Every small, kind thing you do for yourself makes light: a glass of water, an earlier bedtime, ten minutes of music, a walk. Spend that light to bring the island back to life, build a village, and welcome friends who join your journey.

Made for real life. Your plan is built only from what you pick. Missed a day? Nothing is lost, and Lumi is just happy to see you. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
