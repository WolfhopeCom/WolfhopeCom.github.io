Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

In short: your measurements, jobs, customers, materials, prices and business details stay on your device. We have no servers or accounts and never receive any of it. The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show those ads. Pro turns ads off.

Information stored on your device

The app ("ScapeCalc") stores your calculations, saved jobs (including customer names, phone numbers, email addresses and site addresses you enter), materials, prices, business details and settings in its private storage on your device. This information is never sent to us or anyone else. It is included in your device's own backups, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

PDF estimates and sharing

PDF estimates and shared results are created on your device. They go only where you choose to send them, such as Messages, Mail or Files.

Advertising

The free version shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when asked;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information about the ad SDK, such as crashes and performance.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Nothing you type into the app is shared with Google.

Your choices

  • Tracking: you can decline when asked, or change it at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • EU, UK and other consent regions: you're asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change your choice in the app under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Pro: a one-time purchase that turns off all ads and the data collection that comes with them.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app receives only a confirmation from Apple that Pro was purchased. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Children

The app is a general-audience business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
