How the numbers work

How is volume calculated?

Area × finished depth, plus the material's compaction allowance, plus your waste percentage. There are 27 cubic feet in a cubic yard. Weight is cubic yards × the material's weight per loose cubic yard.

What is compaction, and why does crusher run need more?

Dense-graded stone like crusher run or #304 shrinks about 20–25% when it's compacted, so you need about 1.25 loose yards for every finished yard. Screened topsoil settles 10–20% after rain and traffic. Mulch and clean stone such as #57 or pea gravel barely compact, so they default to 0. You can change the allowance on any calculation.

Why is the order bigger than the cubic yards?

Most yards load in half-yard or half-ton steps, so the order is rounded up. You can switch to quarter or whole steps under Ordering. Prices use the rounded-up amount.

The tons don't match my supplier's ticket

Weight per yard depends on the quarry, the gradation and how wet the material is. Ask your supplier for their number and type it into Weight. The app remembers it for that material.

How much paver base do I need?

The paver industry's guidance (ICPI) is 4–6 inches of compacted base for walks and patios and 8–12 inches for driveways, more in cold climates or on poor soil, over 1 inch of bedding sand. Use the Base Depth menu in the Pavers calculator.

How are truckloads counted?

By whichever runs out first, your truck's payload or its bed volume. Mulch usually fills the bed first; stone and soil usually hit the weight limit first. Set your truck or trailer in Settings → Hauling, and always check your vehicle's payload rating.

Pro

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it here?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account. Pro is a one-time purchase and works on all your devices.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. You pay once, and every calculator stays free either way.

Do my customers see my cost and markup?

No. PDF estimates show your prices with markup built in. Your cost and margin appear only in the app.

Should I charge sales tax on labor?

It depends on your state. Ohio, for example, taxes landscaping services as well as materials. Pick what tax applies to on each job (or the default in Settings), and check with your accountant.
