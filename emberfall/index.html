EMBERFALL

MY LORD

Your father is dead, and the throne of Emberfall is yours. Everyone says he died in his sleep. His last letter says otherwise.

Rule a small river city for one year. By day, answer the people who climb to your throne: a starving village, a marriage offer for your daughter, a witch from the fen with a bargain she won't explain, an envoy from Baron Vex, who wants your city. By night, walk the castle, set your taxes, send out your soldiers and build your walls. In high summer, command the defense of the ford yourself. Then your cousin arrives with a will that names him heir, and eleven lords must decide who rules Emberfall. Find your father's killer before they vote.

  • A full year of story in two chapters, with a murder mystery that runs through all of it
  • A playable siege: your preparations and your orders on the night decide the battle
  • Eleven lords to win over before the Midwinter Council, and 20 endings
  • Your family, with conversations that change as the year does
  • Pixel art with real lighting, seasons and weather, and an original soundtrack
  • No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Plays fully offline.
