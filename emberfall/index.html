EMBERFALL

MY LORD

Your father is dead, and the throne of Emberfall is yours. Month after month, petitioners walk the red carpet to your throne: a miller begging grain for a starving village, your wife with a marriage offer for your daughter, a hedge-witch from the fen with a bargain she won't name, and a smiling envoy from Baron Vex, who wants your city.

Every answer costs something. Keep your gold, grain, army and the favor of your people alive through the seasons, and raise granaries, markets and walls with your master mason, because in high summer, Vex's banners appear on the river. Survive him, and the year isn't over: a cousin with a forged will, the King's war, a hard winter, and a council of lords who will decide if you keep your throne.

  • A hand-crafted story with 19 endings across a full year: victory, exile, rebellion, a stolen crown, and stranger fates
  • Choices that come back: alliances, betrayals, curses and secrets carry across the months
  • A living calendar: spring rain, summer sun, autumn leaves and winter snow
  • 16 characters with animated pixel-art portraits and voices of their own
  • A living throne room with torchlight, drifting embers, guards and the castle hound
  • An original soundtrack that shifts from lute by day to bells by night to war drums
  • A Chronicle of your reign and a collection of every ending you've found
  • No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Plays fully offline.
