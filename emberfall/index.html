EMBERFALL

MY LORD

Your father is dead, and the throne of Emberfall is yours. Every day, petitioners walk the red carpet to your throne: a miller begging grain for a starving village, your wife with a marriage offer for your daughter, a hedge-witch from the fen with a bargain she won't name, and a smiling envoy from Baron Vex, who wants your city.

Every answer costs something. Keep your gold, grain, army and the favor of your people alive for nine days, because on the ninth, Vex's banners appear on the river.

  • A hand-crafted story with 13 endings: victory, exile, ruin, a crown, and stranger fates
  • Choices that come back: alliances, betrayals, curses and secrets carry across days
  • 14 characters with animated pixel-art portraits and voices of their own
  • A living throne room with torchlight, drifting embers, guards and the castle hound
  • An original soundtrack that shifts from lute by day to bells by night to war drums
  • A Chronicle of your reign and a collection of every ending you've found
  • No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Plays fully offline.
