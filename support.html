Is my game saved if I leave the app?

Yes. The game is saved continuously and the clock is calculated from timestamps, so it keeps correct time while the app is in the background or the screen is locked. Open the app and tap Resume.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. The purchase works on all your devices.

The buzzer is silent

Check that Sound Effects is on in the app's Settings and the volume is up. Turn on Play in Silent Mode to hear sounds with the ringer switch off.

How do I fix a mistake?

Tap Undo to reverse the last action, as many times as you need. You can also adjust the clock from the clock menu.

Where does my game history go?

History stays on your device only. Swipe a game in History to delete it, or tap Clear to delete everything.

How does Bucket Golf scoring work?

Stroke play, par 3 on every hole. Each swing is one stroke; hitting the bucket finishes the hole; a chip that lands in the bucket takes one stroke off; a hazard adds a one-stroke penalty. Lowest total wins, and the best score on the last hole tees off first.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking can be changed in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU, UK and other regions that require it, the app's Settings → Ad Privacy Choices reopens the consent form. See the Privacy Policy.
