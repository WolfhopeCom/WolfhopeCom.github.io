Curb Appeal

Garden makeover · Match-3 puzzles

Turn neglected yards into dream gardens, one puzzle at a time. Win match-3 levels to earn stars, then spend them on real landscaping jobs: haul off the junk, lay fresh sod, mow perfect stripes, cut and edge beds, plant drifts of flowers, build patios, fire pits, retaining walls and koi ponds.

  • Five properties: a tired front yard, a family backyard, a lakeside cottage, a Japanese hillside garden and a grand estate
  • Jobs follow the order a real crew works in, and you pick the styles: walkways, shrubs, lighting, fences and more
  • Rockets, bombs and rainbow balls, with combos when you swipe them together
  • A boss every ten levels, and hard levels that are clearly marked and pay extra
  • Real landscaping tips to collect in your Field Notes, from a team that does this work for real
  • No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Plays fully offline.
