Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

The short version: Curb Appeal doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the App ("Curb Appeal: Garden Makeover") saves your level, lives, stars, coins, boosters, finished jobs and style choices, the tips you've collected and your settings (sound, music, ambience and vibration). This is stored only in the App's storage on your device, with a backup copy in the App's own settings storage so iOS can't clear it to free space. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the App.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage data. The App doesn't include advertising, analytics or tracking software from other companies, and it works without an internet connection.

Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases of coin packs and bundles. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store under Apple's privacy policy. The App receives only a confirmation from Apple, on your device, that a purchase went through, so it can add the coins to your game. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Children

The App is a general-audience game. Because it doesn't collect personal information from anyone, it doesn't collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
