All-Sports Scoreboard

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

In short: your scores, names, settings and history stay on your device. We have no servers or accounts and never receive any of it. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show those ads. Buying Remove Ads turns ads off.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your settings, team and player names, the game in progress and your game history in its private storage on your device. This information is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app. You can delete history at any time in the History screen.

Advertising

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob on menu screens. Ads are never shown on the scoreboard during a game. To serve, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when asked;
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ad content is limited to material suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

Your choices

  • Tracking: you can decline when asked, or change it at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • EU, UK and other consent regions: you're asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change your choice in the app under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase that turns off all ads and the data collection that comes with them.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

The app is a general-audience scorekeeping tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
