About

Wolfhope Co. is an independent app studio in Ohio, founded by developer Harrison Wolfhope. We make simple, well-made apps and games for iPhone and iPad: the kind you come back to every week, not just once.

So far that means a scoreboard for pickup games and game night, a coding app with a section just for kids, and a pixel-art story game. We design, build and support each one ourselves, and more are on the way.
