About

About Wolfhope

Wolfhope Co. is an independent studio founded by developer Harrison Wolfhope in Ohio. We design, build and support every app ourselves, and we read every message we get.

  • Privacy firstOur apps collect only what they need to work, and each app's privacy policy spells out exactly what that is.
  • Fair pricingClear, honest prices, with free ways to try our apps wherever we can.
  • Made to be usedApps that open fast, work offline wherever they can and stay out of your way.
